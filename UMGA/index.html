02 ABOUT PHISHGUARD

Learn to recognize digital deception.

PhishGuard is a phishing-awareness simulation platform designed as an educational project for the University of Mindanao.

The system demonstrates how deceptive messages, threats, fake rewards, urgency, and suspicious links can be used to influence users into interacting with malicious content.
